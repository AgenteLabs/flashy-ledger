# Code of conduct

This repository publishes the rules a settlement ledger runs on. The people
who read, question and improve those rules are the reason it is open, and
this document is the floor for how they are treated here.

## Expected

- Be direct about the code and courteous to the person. A finding that the
  hash input is wrong is welcome; a remark about who wrote it is not.
- Show your measurement. "This fails" with a reproduction beats "this seems
  off" — and it is how disagreements here get settled.
- Assume the last person had a reason, and ask for it before replacing it.
- Report what happened, including when it is worse than expected.

## Not tolerated

- Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks, in any channel this
  project uses — issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of a thread after being asked to stop.

## Scope

Everywhere this project is discussed under its name, and to anybody
representing it.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the address in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md)
(`security@flashy.network`), which reaches the maintainers, until a dedicated
conduct contact is published. Reports are read by a person, acknowledged
within 72 hours, and handled with the reporter's confidentiality kept.

## Consequences

Maintainers may edit or remove a contribution that breaks this document,
and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban a participant. The
response is proportionate to the behaviour and is explained to the person it
applies to.
